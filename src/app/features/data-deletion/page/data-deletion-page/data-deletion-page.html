YOUR REQUEST

Request removal of information associated with Meta Gateway.

Nexera Group provides a direct process for submitting data deletion requests related to Meta Gateway.

The information required to process a request depends on the integration and the data involved. We may need enough information to identify the relevant business account, integration or platform record.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, private keys, application secrets or other credentials as part of your request.

IMPORTANT

Do not include secrets in your request.

Your deletion request should contain enough information to identify the relevant account or integration, but it should never include sensitive authentication information.

  • Passwords
  • Meta access tokens
  • Application secrets
  • Private encryption keys
  • API secrets
  • Authentication credentials

AFTER SUBMISSION

What happens after we receive your request?

01

Request review

We review the information provided and determine which integration or records relate to the request.

02

Identity or authorization verification

Additional verification may be required before information associated with an account or organization can be deleted.

03

Data handling

Relevant information may be deleted, anonymized or otherwise removed from active processing where appropriate.

04

Response

We may contact you if additional information is required or to confirm the outcome of the request.

DATA RETENTION

Some information may have different retention requirements.

A deletion request does not necessarily mean every record can always be removed immediately.

Certain information may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, system integrity, legal obligations, dispute resolution or other legitimate operational requirements.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted, anonymized or otherwise removed according to the applicable operational requirements.
